# Systems of Differential Equations
Back to [Index](../Index.md) · Chapter 1.3

A system couples several unknown functions. The classic example is the **Lotka–Volterra predator–prey system**:
$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{dx}{dt} &= ax - \alpha xy \\
\frac{dy}{dt} &= -cy + \gamma xy
\end{align*}
$$
Here $x(t)$ is the prey population and $y(t)$ is the predator population.

A second-order equation can be rewritten as a first-order system. For example, $x'' = -kx'$ becomes $x' = v,\ v' = -kv$ (see [WB1 P01 - Car Slowing Down to a Stop](../Workbook%201/WB1%20P01%20-%20Car%20Slowing%20Down%20to%20a%20Stop.md)).
